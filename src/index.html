Quillbound Sheets

Play and build your character on your phone or tablet, with your MPMB character sheet and its own automation.

Reading the PDF

Works with MPMB's 2024 (5.5e) and 2014 (5e) character sheets, Colorful and Printer Friendly, including books you imported in Acrobat. Your file stays on this device.

Version 0.1 test build. Your character and add-on content are saved on this device as you go; Download PDF (Sheet tab) writes the character into your sheet for Acrobat. MPMB's dialogs and questions are shown to you, as in Acrobat. Quillbound Sheets works with MPMB's character sheets; it isn't made by MPMB.

            Options

              Settings

              Look

              The app's look, after MPMB's two sheet designs. Colorful takes the sheet's colour (Sheet → Pages → Change → Color Theme options). Kept on this device.

              MPMB's dialogs

              How the app shows MPMB's dialogs and questions. Changing it reopens your character (it is saved first). Kept on this device.

              Install

              Put the app on your home screen. It opens on its own, without the browser bar, and works without a connection once opened.

              Backups

              A backup is the character written into your sheet's PDF, as Download PDF makes it: open it in the app (Open another sheet) or in Acrobat.

              MPMB

              MPMB got stuck

              MPMB's scripts stopped responding during a change, so the app restarted the sheet and reopened your character as it was saved just before that change.

              A known cause is a bug in MPMB's own code: removing something whose text is on the third (or later) Notes page makes it loop forever. Clearing out the Notes pages prevents that; you can copy their text first.

              Clear out Notes pages

              This removes all Notes pages from your PDF (MPMB's own page removal). The Notes on the third page and on the Notes card stay. This can't be undone.

              The Notes pages are empty.
