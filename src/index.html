MPMB Sheet App

Play and build your MPMB character on your phone or tablet, using the sheet's own automation.

Reading the PDF

Works with MPMB's 2024 (5.5e) character sheets, including books you imported in Acrobat. Your file stays on this device.

Version 0.1 test build. Your character and add-on content are saved on this device as you go (not into the PDF yet). Some of MPMB's pop-up questions are still answered with their default choices.
